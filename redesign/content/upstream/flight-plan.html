The Flight Plan.

LANDING has a direction, but it does not sell imaginary destinations. This plan records what has been completed, what is being built and what the project may become when the conditions are real.

This is a direction, not a promise.

No artificial deadlines. No guaranteed listings. No fictional utility. A phase changes status only when the progress behind it actually exists.

Five phases.
One grounded direction.

Not a roadmap to Mars. A transparent path from a deployed Solana token to a community with its own identity and a meaningful role in what comes next.

Completed

Touchdown

The foundation exists on-chain and can be independently checked.

  • LANDING launched on Solana Mainnet
  • Fixed supply of 404,000,000 LANDING
  • Mint and freeze authorities permanently disabled
  • Token allocations and project wallets published
  • Orca liquidity pool activated and Jupiter routing available
  • Website, documentation and official channels launched
Advanced

Build the Runway

Make LANDING easier to verify, discover and access without manufacturing activity.

  • Expand visibility across reputable trackers and wallets
  • First centralized exchange listing announced: LANDING/USDT on Poloniex
  • Keep token information and public documentation accurate
  • Develop editorial, exchange and ecosystem visibility
  • Continue evaluating future trading venues individually
  • Grow without fake holders, wash trading or coordinated shilling
In progress

Bring More Landers Aboard

Turn attention into an active community rather than a temporary audience.

  • Telegram, Discord and Reddit community spaces established
  • Develop recurring community content across official channels
  • Encourage community-created memes, articles and videos
  • Explore collaborations aligned with LANDING's identity
  • Create ways for Landers to participate without becoming shillers
  • Pursue larger opportunities only when organic traction supports them
Direction

Beyond the Landing

Use the project's reach and resources only when there is something real worth building.

  • Evaluate practical uses for the Community allocation
  • Invite Landers to propose and shape future initiatives
  • Define transparent criteria before resources are committed
  • Support selected experiments connected to the blockchain world
  • Document completed initiatives instead of announcing imaginary ones
Long-term direction

The Lander Era

A holder owns a token. A Lander belongs to something.

The long-term direction of LANDING is not to create another crowd watching a price chart. It is to build a recognizable community with its own identity, culture and voice.

  • Lander identity, Lander Code and Certified Lander system established
  • Grow recognition and adoption of the Lander identity
  • Give active community members opportunities to contribute
  • Develop initiatives shaped with the community
  • Recognize genuine participation, creativity and long-term presence
  • Turn LANDING from a token people hold into a culture people represent
You don't just hold LANDING.
You become a Lander.

No dates invented for marketing. No destinations guaranteed. LANDING moves forward when the next step is real.
