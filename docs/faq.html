01 / THE USEFUL QUESTIONS

01What is LANDING?+

LANDING is an independent Solana memecoin built around transparency, irony and realistic expectations.

02What blockchain is LANDING on?+

LANDING is an SPL token on Solana Mainnet.

03What is the official LANDING token address?+

The official LANDING mint on Solana Mainnet is DLGLMB3imJqnAwKjxY8ZFXoF7CPfTbCNL3j3Ag5M5vGZ.

04What is the LANDING maximum supply?+

The LANDING maximum supply is 404,000,000 tokens, with 6 decimals.

05Can more LANDING tokens be minted?+

Mint authority is disabled. No additional tokens can be minted through that authority.

06Can the token authority freeze LANDING wallets?+

The token’s freeze authority is disabled. It cannot be used to freeze LANDING token accounts.

07Is liquidity locked?+

No. Liquidity is not locked.

08Who controls the allocations?+

Significant allocations are controlled by project wallets. Community and Treasury allocations are not tokens already distributed to independent holders.

09How can LANDING be bought?+

Through Jupiter with a compatible Solana wallet, or LANDING / USDT on Poloniex. Review the token, quote, warnings and fees.

10What is the LANDING Wall?+

A public message wall with a 140-character limit. Posting here costs 5,000 LANDING plus the Solana network fee through a compatible Solana wallet. Browsing does not require a wallet.

11What is Certified Lander?+

A verified-swap community experience on the Certified Lander page. The existing registry verifies an eligible finalized Jupiter swap, then issues or retrieves one permanent ID per wallet and enables the card download. It is not an NFT and has no promised utility.

12Is LANDING an investment?+

LANDING is openly speculative. Buying it does not create an entitlement to profits, products or guaranteed outcomes, and you can lose the amount you spend.

13Does LANDING promise returns?+

No. LANDING does not promise returns or a price target.

14Do I need tokens to join Discord?+

No. Tokens are not required to join the Discord community.

15Are all dashboard figures live?+

No. Immutable facts, original allocation and current readings are separated. Source timestamps, partial coverage and unavailable market data are labelled.

16Who operates LANDING?+

LANDING is operated by Michele Scalini — Founder & Project Operator, with Furqan — Growth & Community Manager. See the LANDING team.

17Where is the official LANDING community?+

Discord is the main LANDING community hub. Official Telegram, Reddit, LinkedIn and X links are listed on the LANDING Community page.
