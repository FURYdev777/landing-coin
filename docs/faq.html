LANDING/FAQ
FAQNo hedging

Straight answers, including the bad ones.

These are the questions that actually get asked, answered the way we would answer them in the Discord rather than the way a disclaimer would.

01 / Answers

The list.

Is LANDING going to go up?

We do not know, and anyone who tells you they do is guessing or selling. LANDING is a speculative memecoin with no revenue and no assets behind it. The realistic outcome for most memecoins is that they end up near zero. That sentence appears on several pages of this site on purpose.

Does LANDING promise any returns?

No. There is no price target and no promised return. Buying LANDING gives you no claim on profits, products or any outcome, and you can lose what you spend.

Is the liquidity locked?

No. It is not locked, and we say so on every page that touches the subject rather than leaving you to find out. Nothing mechanically prevents liquidity being removed. The project wallets are published by address so you can watch them instead of trusting a sentence about them.

What is the official mint address?

DLGLMB3imJqnAwKjxY8ZFXoF7CPfTbCNL3j3Ag5M5vGZ, on Solana mainnet. Check it before you buy anywhere. A token with the same name and a different address is not LANDING.

How do I buy LANDING?

Two ways. Swap through Jupiter from your own Solana wallet, or trade LANDING/USDT on Poloniex. Either way, check the mint address, the quote and the fees before you approve anything.

Can more LANDING ever be created?

No. The mint authority was disabled at deployment, which is permanent and cannot be undone by us or by anyone who takes over the project later. Supply is fixed at 404,000,000 forever. This is a field on the mint account, not a claim on a website. Open the token on an explorer and read it yourself.

Can my wallet be frozen?

No. The freeze authority was also disabled. Nobody, including us, can stop you moving or selling your tokens.

How much of the supply does the project hold?

A meaningful share. The Community, Treasury, Liquidity reserve and Creator allocations are all project controlled, and all four addresses are published on the Transparency page with explorer links. The Community allocation being called community describes what it is set aside for, not who holds it today. We would rather say that here than have you discover it and assume we hid it.

Why is the market data sometimes blank?

Because the third-party market source is not answering. When that happens the dashboard shows the field as unavailable instead of filling it with the last number it saw. A stale figure presented as live is worse than no figure. Unavailable means unavailable, and it is never an estimate.

What is the LANDING Wall?

A public message wall on Solana. A mark is one line of up to 140 characters and costs 5,000 LANDING plus the network fee. Reading the Wall is free and needs no wallet.

Is the Certified Lander card an NFT?

No. It is not a token, not tradeable, and not a claim on anything. It is a permanent Lander ID and a card you can download, issued after an eligible swap through the Certified Lander page. Anyone offering to buy your card is running a different operation.

Does connecting my wallet put my funds at risk?

Connecting lets a site read public balances. Nothing moves until you approve a transaction in your own wallet: 5,000 LANDING for a mark on the Wall, or the swap you choose on the Lander page. Nothing on this site will ever ask you to approve a token allowance, and nobody from this project will ever ask for a recovery phrase. There is no support process that needs one.

Is there a second contract, a v2 or a migration?

No. There is one mint address, it is printed on several pages here, and it will not change. Any announcement of a new contract is an impersonation attempt, however convincing the account looks.

Who is behind the project?

Michele Scalini, who founded LANDING and operates it, with Furqan on brand design and community. The Team page says so plainly rather than listing departments that do not exist.

Why is the supply 404,000,000?

Because 404 is the error code for something that was not found, which seemed like the right number for a token whose whole position is that the moon is not there. That is the entire reason. There is no tokenomics model behind it and we are not going to invent one.

Do I need LANDING to join the community?

No. You do not need any tokens to join the Discord, which is the main community hub. Every official channel is listed on the Community page.

How do I report an impersonator?

Email contact@landingcoin.fun, or post it in the open channels so other people see it too. Scam accounts work by catching the people who have not heard yet, so speed matters more than process.
