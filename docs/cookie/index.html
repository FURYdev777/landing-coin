SITE INFORMATION

Cookie &
Local Storage.

A short description of the browser storage used by the current LANDING website.

First-party functional storage.

The site uses browser storage to keep some features usable between page loads.

Motion preference
landing:motion-paused may be stored locally when you pause decorative motion.
FUNCTIONAL
Certified Lander
The Lander page can store a pending transaction signature and a previously verified wallet address in localStorage so verification or card restoration can continue.
FUNCTIONAL

Third-party services.

Some features open or load services LANDING does not control.

Jupiter, Poloniex, Solana wallets, explorers, market trackers, Google Fonts and other external services may apply their own storage, cookie and privacy practices when their services are loaded or opened. Their policies are independent from LANDING.

Your controls.

Browser storage can generally be cleared through your browser's site-data settings.

The current LANDING static site does not rely on functional localStorage to represent token ownership or on-chain truth. Clearing local storage does not alter Solana transactions or token balances.
