LANDING / SOLANA MAINNETGRAVITY: ACTIVE
CURRENT POLICY

Last updated: 19 October 2026. This page describes the current LANDING website and services.

1. Who is responsible

LANDING is an independent project operated by Michele Scalini — LANDING Founder & Project Operator, the controller for the project processing described here. Contact: contact@landingcoin.fun. Official website: landingcoin.fun.

This policy covers LANDING website functions and project infrastructure. Wallet applications, exchanges and other external services make their own decisions about their services and data. Their policies also apply when you use them.

2. Wallets and public transaction data

When you connect a Solana wallet or enter an address for a lookup, LANDING processes the public wallet address to provide the function you request. LANDING never receives or stores private keys or seed phrases. You approve and sign transactions in your own wallet software.

LANDING reads public Solana data, including wallet and token-account addresses, signatures, token movements, balances, memo content, slots and transaction times. Sources are the public blockchain through RPC infrastructure and the transaction signature supplied by the swap interface. These reads verify Wall payments and swaps, retrieve or assign Lander IDs, and provide transparency information.

Blockchain information is public and may be permanently recorded independently of LANDING. Wallet addresses are not necessarily anonymous: activity and messages may be linked to a person. LANDING cannot modify or erase information already recorded on the public Solana network.

3. LANDING Wall

Wall messages are intentionally public. Posting puts the message in a transaction memo associated with the paying wallet. The interface can show the message, wallet address, signature and transaction time. Do not post private information about yourself or others.

The current Wall service reads qualifying transactions from Solana rather than keeping a separate message database. Information is processed to verify the payment and display the Wall. Messages may be hidden from LANDING’s interface for moderation, abuse prevention or legal reasons. Hiding a message does not erase its transaction or public memo.

4. Certified Lander registry and card

The Certified Lander registry stores a permanent progressive Lander ID, public wallet address, qualifying transaction signature, quantity of LANDING received, verified balance at issuance, confirmation slot and creation timestamp in the project’s Cloudflare D1 database. One ID is associated with each registered wallet so the same record can be retrieved later.

The existing lookup returns registry details to anyone who supplies the wallet address; it does not require proof that the requester owns that wallet. Treat registry information as publicly retrievable. Certified Lander is not an anonymous service.

The display name you enter for a card is used in the browser to render and download the card. The current application does not send that name or the generated card image to the LANDING registry. Local recovery storage is described in the Cookie & Local Storage Policy.

5. Technical information and contact

Hosting, CDN, RPC, security and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request paths and parameters, timestamps, browser or network information and security logs to deliver and protect their services. A wallet address or signature sent in a request may therefore also appear in technical records.

The registry’s published configuration enables operational logging. Its error-handling code can log a transaction signature or wallet address together with an error. Technical/security logs are retained according to operational necessity and the configuration of the relevant infrastructure provider. No fixed retention period is specified here.

If you email contact@landingcoin.fun, the address, message and any information you voluntarily supply are used to answer your request and maintain necessary correspondence. Do not send private keys or seed phrases.

6. Purposes and legal bases

  • Requested services: processing needed to perform a service you expressly request, such as Wall verification or Lander issue/retrieval, is based on performance of that service under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where applicable.
  • Operation and protection: necessary website operation, public-data transparency, content moderation, fraud or abuse prevention, technical security and ordinary correspondence rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f), balanced against the rights of affected people.
  • Legal obligations: Article 6(1)(c) applies where a specific legal obligation requires processing. It is not a blanket reason to retain everything.

Connecting a wallet, signing a transaction or opening a third-party service is not treated as general consent to advertising or unrelated processing. LANDING does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural profiling. Eligibility checks are automated transaction and balance checks; they do not create financial, voting or investment rights. Contact the operator if you believe a result is incorrect.

You can read ordinary pages without connecting a wallet. If you do not provide the public wallet or signature needed for a requested function, that function cannot verify or retrieve the relevant record.

7. Recipients and independent services

  • Cloudflare: project API hosting, registry database and operational security. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Solana and RPC providers: public transaction submission and retrieval, balances and network verification. The service can use a configured RPC provider and public RPC fallbacks.
  • Jupiter and your chosen wallet: the activated swap interface, wallet connection and transaction execution. See Jupiter’s privacy policy and your wallet provider’s notices.
  • Poloniex: its independent account and exchange services when you follow the Poloniex route. LANDING does not receive your exchange password or account verification documents through this website.
  • CDNs and linked services: jsDelivr and esm.sh deliver wallet/card libraries; explorers, market-data providers and community platforms operate their own services when used. Transparency also obtains public market information through its backend.

The private review environment additionally uses ChatGPT Sites access and hosting infrastructure. Its account/authentication processing is separate from the project’s wallet registry. External providers are not controlled by LANDING and have their own terms and privacy policies.

8. International processing

Public blockchain data is distributed globally. Infrastructure providers and independent third-party services may process data outside the EEA. Those providers are responsible for the safeguards applicable to their own processing or contractual relationship. LANDING remains responsible for the obligations applicable to its own processing. This policy does not claim that LANDING has executed any specific Standard Contractual Clauses, data-processing agreement, certification or international-transfer arrangement. Cloudflare describes its privacy and international-processing practices in its current privacy documentation; that documentation is not a statement that a particular contractual safeguard has been executed by LANDING.

9. How long information is kept

  • Public blockchain: public Solana blockchain data is outside LANDING’s technical control and may remain publicly available permanently.
  • Lander registry: Certified Lander registry records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the registry, preserve permanent Lander-ID continuity, protect the integrity of the service and meet applicable legal obligations. The permanent-ID design does not override applicable data-protection rights.
  • Technical/security records: logs are retained according to operational necessity and the configuration of the relevant infrastructure provider.
  • Correspondence: contact correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the communication and maintain legitimate records.
  • Browser storage: follows the removal behaviour described in the storage policy; browser clearing does not delete a registry record.

10. Your rights

Where GDPR applies, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, subject to the conditions for each right. Send requests to contact@landingcoin.fun. Proportionate identity verification may be needed; private keys and seed phrases are never required. Requests are handled within the periods required by applicable law.

Deletion requests are assessed under applicable law. LANDING can assess requests about its own registry, correspondence, interface and records, but cannot erase information already recorded on the Solana blockchain or rewrite its public ledger. That technical limit does not remove rights concerning copies or processing that LANDING controls. A request will be assessed on its facts, including any duty to retain information.

You may lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, including the Italian Data Protection Authority where applicable.

11. Changes and related policies

This policy may be updated when features, providers or processing change. The date above identifies the current version. Read the Cookie & Local Storage Policy and Terms of Service alongside it.
