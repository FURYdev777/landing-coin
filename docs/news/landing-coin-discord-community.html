The official LANDING Coin Discord community is now open.

Discord gives LANDING another place to bring project information and community conversations together without replacing the channels that already exist. Telegram remains useful for fast discussion, Reddit for longer-form posts, and Bitcointalk for the project's public ANN history. Discord adds a more structured environment where topics can live in their own spaces.

Join LANDING on Discord

Why add Discord?

LANDING has gradually expanded beyond a token page and a trading pair. The project now includes the LANDING Wall, the Certified Lander experience, public transparency pages, news and several community channels. As that grows, a single fast-moving chat is not always the best place for every kind of conversation.

Discord makes it easier to separate project information, general discussion and future community activities while keeping everything under the same LANDING identity.

A community space, not an activity counter

The goal is not to create dozens of empty channels or manufacture the appearance of a busy community. The server is designed to give LANDING a useful structure and then allow participation to grow naturally.

That follows the same principle used elsewhere in the project: build something real first, make it public when it is ready, and avoid pretending that a bigger number automatically means a stronger community.

LANDING's official community channels

The project can now be followed and discussed through several official spaces:

  • Discord — structured community discussions and project spaces;
  • Telegram — fast community conversation and updates;
  • Reddit — posts, discussions and longer-form community content;
  • Bitcointalk — the public LANDING announcement thread.

Official project links are also maintained in the LANDING Media Kit and on the main website.

Come for the meme. Stay for the conversation.

No LANDING tokens are required to join the server. Holding the token is not a membership requirement, and joining Discord does not imply any financial benefit or promised reward.

It is simply another place to follow the project, talk with the community and see what LANDING builds next.
