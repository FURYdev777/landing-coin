LANDING/THE WALL IS LIVE. WHAT COMES NEXT?
PROJECT DEVELOPMENT / MISSION LOG

The Wall is live.
What comes next?

A live public Wall, and a merchandise store that remains a future concept.

PRIVATE REVIEW / SOLANA MAINNETGRAVITY: ACTIVE
2026-09-25PROJECT DEVELOPMENTLANDING / PUBLISHED RECORD

The LANDING Wall is now live. It is a simple community feature built around the LANDING token: users can leave a public post-it style message on the wall after making the required LANDING payment on Solana.

That may sound deliberately modest. It is. LANDING was never built around a promise of revolutionary utility. The project has consistently avoided inventing features merely to make a memecoin look more important than it is. Instead, the goal is to test small uses that are understandable, transparent and actually connected to the community.

What is the LANDING Wall?

The LANDING Wall is a public community space on the official LANDING Coin website. A post costs 5,000 LANDING, and the payment is verified through Solana before the message is accepted.

The result is intentionally simple: one payment, one public message, one small mark left behind. The Wall does not claim to create financial value or transform LANDING into a utility token in the regulatory or technical sense. It is simply a token-powered community interaction.

For LANDING, that distinction matters. A feature should exist because people can actually use it, not because a roadmap needs another impressive-looking bullet point.

Why experiment with LANDING token utility?

Memecoins are usually valued for culture, community and market attention rather than traditional product utility. LANDING does not pretend otherwise. But that does not mean the token has to remain disconnected from everything the community does.

The Wall explores a narrower idea: using LANDING as a participation layer inside the LANDING ecosystem.

That approach can include experiences where the token has an obvious role:

  • community interactions such as the LANDING Wall;
  • identity features such as the Lander concept and verified community cards;
  • future physical merchandise connected to LANDING's visual identity;
  • other small features where paying or holding LANDING has a clear purpose.

The principle remains the same: build something first, explain exactly what it does, and avoid assigning imaginary value to it.

Next idea: a LANDING merchandise store

One of the concepts currently being explored is an official LANDING merchandise store. The idea is to turn parts of the project's identity — the LANDING coin, the Lander character, the anti-moon language and the visual style of the project — into physical products.

Potential items include small collectibles and branded products such as keychains, apparel, accessories and other LANDING-themed gadgets. A print-on-demand model is being considered so products can be produced after an order instead of requiring the project to maintain large physical inventory.

The more interesting part is the payment flow. The long-term objective is to evaluate whether customers could place an order using LANDING tokens, while the backend handles the conversion required to pay the fulfillment provider in conventional currency.

Important: the merchandise store and LANDING payment flow are still future project concepts. They are not live today, and technical, commercial and compliance details still need to be completed before any launch is announced.

From speculation to participation

LANDING remains a speculative memecoin. A Wall, a community card or a future merchandise store does not change that fact.

What these features can change is the relationship between the token and the community around it. Instead of LANDING existing only as an asset that moves between wallets and markets, it can also become a way to participate in small parts of the project's culture.

That is a much narrower claim than the word “utility” often carries in crypto, and deliberately so.

The project does not need to claim that LANDING powers a financial revolution. It is enough for LANDING to power things that make sense inside LANDING.

Building utility without changing the identity

Any future feature will continue to follow the same communication principles used elsewhere on the project:

  • no guaranteed returns;
  • no invented demand;
  • no fake partnerships;
  • no utility announced before it actually exists;
  • clear separation between live features and future plans.

The LANDING Transparency page will continue to document verifiable token and market information, while the LANDING News section will publish future developments when they become concrete.

For now, the Wall is live. The store is an idea being developed. That is exactly how LANDING intends to communicate progress: what exists, what is being explored, and nothing in between disguised as certainty.

Everyone is going to the moon. We are finding things to do after landing.
