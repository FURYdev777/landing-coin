LANDING is listed on Poloniex.

The published LANDING / USDT listing announcement and official market route.

LANDING / SOLANA MAINNETGRAVITY: ACTIVE
2026-09-21EXCHANGELANDING / PUBLISHED RECORD

LANDING Coin has reached a new milestone: LANDING is listed on Poloniex. The LANDING/USDT spot market is scheduled to open on September 21, 2026 at 12:00 UTC.

For a project built around a deliberately simple idea — “Everyone is going to the moon. We are landing.” — the Poloniex listing adds a new way for users to access and trade LANDING while the project continues to operate on Solana.

LANDING/USDT on Poloniex

The listing introduces a centralized LANDING/USDT market on Poloniex alongside LANDING's existing on-chain presence and Solana liquidity.

The official LANDING mint address remains:

DLGLMB3imJqnAwKjxY8ZFXoF7CPfTbCNL3j3Ag5M5vGZ

Users should always verify the official mint address before interacting with LANDING or any third-party platform claiming to support the token.

What the Poloniex listing changes

Until now, LANDING's market activity has primarily developed through the Solana ecosystem. The addition of a centralized exchange gives traders another access point, with LANDING quoted directly against USDT.

The listing does not change LANDING's fixed token supply of 404,000,000 LANDING. Mint authority and freeze authority remain disabled.

It also does not change the project's communication policy. LANDING does not promise returns, predetermined price targets or guaranteed outcomes. Developments are announced when they actually happen.

A milestone, not a destination

The Poloniex listing is an important step for LANDING, but it is not presented as an end point. The project will continue to develop its community, transparency tools, market infrastructure and public documentation while monitoring what happens after centralized trading begins.

Future exchange updates, project milestones and market-related announcements will be published in the official LANDING News section.
