LANDING/Community
CommunityOpen

Four rooms. Different jobs.

We did not spread across every platform to look bigger. Each of these is for something specific, and knowing which is which saves you reading all four.

01 / Rooms

Pick the right one.

Day to day

Discord

The main room. Where announcements land first and where anyone actually building something ends up.

Fast and loud

Telegram

Shorter, quicker, messier. Good for a question you want answered in the next few minutes.

Longer form

Reddit

For things worth more than one paragraph. Criticism of the project included, and it stays up.

Public face

X

Announcements and the jokes. Lowest signal, highest reach, and the account most likely to be impersonated.

Formal

LinkedIn

The company page. Mostly there so the project is findable by people who check these things before participating.

Direct

Email

contact@landingcoin.fun. Slower than all of the above and the right channel for anything that needs a record.

02 / Rules

Short list, applied evenly.

Most communities publish rules nobody reads. These are the four that actually get used.

No price promises, from anyone

Including from people with a role colour. Nobody here knows where it is going, and anyone who says they do is either guessing or selling.

No DMing people a contract address

We never do it, so anyone doing it is not us. This is the single most common way holders get drained.

Criticism stays

Saying the project is going to fail is allowed. It might. Deleting that would make every other message worthless.

No seed phrases. Not ever, for any reason

No moderator, admin or support account will ask for one. There is no legitimate process that needs it.

03 / Safety

How to tell it is really us.

We will never message you first

Not on Discord, Telegram, X or anywhere else. If an account claiming to be the team opens a conversation with you, that is the signal, regardless of how convincing the profile looks.

There is one mint address and it never changes

It is DLGLMB3imJqnAwKjxY8ZFXoF7CPfTbCNL3j3Ag5M5vGZ and it is printed on several pages of this site. There is no migration, no v2, and no new contract.

Support does not exist as a DM

There is no support team that handles wallet problems privately. Ask in the open channel, where other people can see the answer and correct it if it is wrong.

Links on this site are the real ones

If you are not sure whether a server or account is genuine, come back here and use the link rather than the one you were sent.

Come in, then.

Discord is where most of it happens. The rest is optional.
