01 / PROJECT IDENTITY

Same risk.
Different attitude.

LANDING is an independently created memecoin on Solana Mainnet. It does not try to make a memecoin look like a revolution. It tries to make the proposition more explicit, more verifiable and less dependent on fictional promises.

Everyone is going to the moon. We are landing.

What LANDING is.

The project is intentionally simple about its current nature.

An openly speculative memecoin.

LANDING is described as a memecoin rather than wrapped in a fictional utility story.

An independent project.

LANDING was started by Michele Scalini and documents its current project structure publicly.

A transparency experiment.

Supply, authorities, allocations, project wallets and liquidity information are published so they can be checked independently.

The LANDING way.

Communication rules matter because memecoin marketing often rewards certainty before evidence.

What LANDING does not claim.

Transparency does not reduce the financial risk of holding a memecoin.

No guaranteed utility, cash flow, yield, redemption right or mechanism that guarantees market value.

LANDING does not claim to represent equity, guarantee returns or provide a predetermined path to value. Market value may be highly volatile and may fall to zero.
