SITE INFORMATION

Privacy
Policy.

LANDING is built around public blockchain infrastructure. Some information used by project features is therefore public by design.

Public blockchain information.

Solana wallet addresses, token balances and transaction signatures are public blockchain data. When you use wallet-based LANDING features, the site may read this public information to perform the requested function.

LANDING Wall.

Wall messages are submitted as part of a Solana transaction and can be reconstructed from public chain history. The site displays the message, transaction signature, wallet information needed by the feature and transaction time when available. A message can be hidden from the website for moderation reasons, but the underlying blockchain transaction cannot be deleted by LANDING.

Certified Lander.

The Certified Lander registry records the wallet, qualifying transaction signature, verified LANDING balance at issue time, confirmed slot, creation time and progressive Lander ID needed to provide and restore the feature. One permanent Lander ID is assigned per verified wallet.

Browser storage.

The site may store functional state locally in your browser, including motion preference and Certified Lander verification/restoration state. See the Cookie & Local Storage page for details.

Third-party services.

Wallet providers, Jupiter, Poloniex, Solana infrastructure, explorers, market trackers, fonts and other external services are independent from LANDING and may process information under their own privacy policies when you use or load those services.

Contact.

If you contact LANDING by email, the information you send is used to receive and respond to the communication.
