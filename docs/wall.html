Everyone who made it down.

Pay 5,000 LANDING, leave one line, and it stays on Solana. Reading is free. There is no ranking by balance and no way to buy a better spot. It only records that you were here.

02 / Join

Leave your mark

One line of up to 140 characters, text only. Kept short on purpose: a wall of essays is a forum nobody reads.

Your mark Connect a wallet to post
0 / 140

A mark costs 5,000 LANDING plus a small SOL network fee. The payment and your message go in one Solana transaction, so read the wallet request before you approve it. LANDING bought on an exchange has to be withdrawn to your own Solana wallet first.

03 / Rules

What the wall is and is not.

Not a leaderboard

Marks appear in the order they were left. A larger balance does not move you up, because that would make the wall a scoreboard and scoreboards attract a worse crowd.

Not a giveaway

There is nothing to win here. If someone tells you posting on the wall qualifies you for an airdrop, they are lying and probably about to ask for your seed phrase.

One payment, no allowance

A mark is a single transfer of 5,000 LANDING with your message attached. It never asks for a spending allowance, and any prompt that does is not from us.

Public for good

Links are not allowed. A message with abuse or illegal content can be hidden from this page, but the transaction stays on-chain and cannot be deleted. Write something you are happy to keep.
